How organization certification works

A process,not a badge.

  1. Step 01 / 05

    Register

    Create your organization profile and begin your certification journey. If you are unsure where to begin, the free analysis gives you a first view.

  2. Step 02 / 05

    Evaluation

    Your practices are reviewed against the CNESS framework, its six pillars and the certification standards. Claims need relevant evidence behind them.

  3. Step 03 / 05

    Get certified

    When you meet the required standards, you earn a certification level and your Trust Profile is updated to show it.

  4. Step 04 / 05

    Amplify your brand

    Share your recognition through CNESS platforms, badges and the Global Directory, so customers and partners can check it for themselves.

  5. Step 05 / 05

    Grow your impact

    Keep improving, share best practices with other organizations and keep records that support your renewal.

Product Certification

Seven pillars.700 points.

  1. Product pillar 01 / 07

    Product Integrity & Safety

    Built to fulfil its intended purpose. Designed to reduce avoidable harm. Governed responsibly as it changes.

  2. Product pillar 02 / 07

    Human Value & Wellbeing

    Created with people in mind. Respectful of dignity, access and wellbeing.

  3. Product pillar 03 / 07

    Ethical Sourcing & Responsible Value Chain

    Know where it comes from. Choose partners with care. Create through fair practice.

  4. Product pillar 04 / 07

    Environmental Responsibility & Sustainability

    Use resources with restraint. Reduce waste and avoidable impact. Consider the whole lifecycle.

  5. Product pillar 05 / 07

    Quality Performance & Lifecycle Value

    Do what it promises. Deliver consistently and reliably. Learn, improve and endure.

  6. Product pillar 06 / 07

    Customer Trust & Support

    Speak clearly and truthfully. Make choice and use understandable. Stand by the customer after the sale.

  7. Product pillar 07 / 07

    Utility & Pain-Point Solutions

    Create value beyond the transaction. Understand the wider consequences. Improve impact with purpose.

Questions

Before you begin.

Still unsure which path fits? Talk to the team

What is CNESS Certification?

CNESS Certification recognizes who your organization is, what it stands for and how it creates value across six pillars: Conscious Leadership, Human-Centric Value & Team Cohesion, Products & Services, Customers & Clients, Community & Conscious Social Impact, and Brand Vision & Strategic Collaborations.

Is the free analysis the same as certification?

No. The analysis gives an initial view based on your website and public information. Certification is a separate process with eligibility checks, a complete assessment, evidence review and a certification decision. A low analysis score highlights areas to improve; it does not automatically make you ineligible.

Do my existing certifications affect eligibility?

No. Existing certifications do not affect your eligibility. They can complement the CNESS framework and add value to your organization’s profile.

Does a Premium directory plan improve my result?

No. Free and Premium use the same certification standards. Premium adds help promoting your business; it does not change your assessment score or the decision to award certification.

Does CNESSWORKS test products in a laboratory?

No. CNESSWORKS evaluates the transparency, relevance and validity of the documents you submit, such as supplier declarations, ISO or GMP certificates, traceability records and scientific reports for product claims.

What can stop an organization from proceeding?

Certification cannot proceed if your organization’s identity or assessment scope cannot be established, legal eligibility cannot be confirmed, claims are unsupported by evidence, the required assessment is declined, or a critical safety or harm concern remains unresolved.
