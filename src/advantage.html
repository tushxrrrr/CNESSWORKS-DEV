Trust Profile

Give people one place to learn about your business

A Trust Profile brings your CNESS recognition and business practices together on one page. It shows which areas have been reviewed and your current recognition status.

Create Your Trust Profile →

How CNESS helps

  • Bring your CNESS recognition details together.
  • Explain what has been reviewed.
  • Help customers understand your business before they contact you.

How you can use it

  • Share your profile with customers and business partners.
  • Use it to answer questions about the way you work.

What you gain

  • A clear reference people can check.
  • A place to share good practices from your organization.

What this means for your business

People can see the actions behind your commitments and decide whether your business meets their needs.

Spend more time discussing customer needs

A customer may want to know what your recognition covers before discussing a purchase. Your profile gives them a place to check. This can reduce repeated questions and leave more time to talk about how you can help.

Organization Certification

Turn your values into everyday action

CNESS reviews six areas of how your organization works. This review, called an assessment, helps you understand your progress and where you can improve. Certification is awarded when you meet the required CNESS standards.

Apply for Organization Certification →

How CNESS helps

  • Review how your organization works.
  • Identify what needs attention.
  • Understand your current stage of progress.

How you can use it

  • Choose improvements based on the review.
  • Keep track of progress as you work towards renewal.

What you gain

  • A way to show how your values guide your work.
  • Clearer information for customers, employees and business partners.
  • Extra listing features through the Global Directory's Premium plan.

What this means for your business

Help people understand what your organization stands for and what you are doing about it.

Help buyers check how you work

Some buyers ask how you treat people, care for the environment and run your organization. Your certification can help answer questions about the areas reviewed. Each buyer decides whether the certification meets their own requirements.

Aspiring · Level One

Take your first steps

Aspiring is the foundation level. It focuses on understanding where you are now and choosing practical improvements.

Apply for Organization Certification →

How CNESS helps

  • Understand your starting point.
  • Choose actions you can manage.
  • Keep records that show your progress.

How you can use it

  • Put your first improvements into practice.
  • Review what works and prepare for your next review.

What you gain

  • A clearer direction for improving your organization.
  • A way to support your commitments with real examples.

What this means for your business

Start making changes you can explain and build on over time.

Understand the three levels

Aspiring: build your foundation. Inspired: show the improvements you have made. Luminary: show well-established practices and share what you have learned. Begin with the free Analyze tool to explore where your organization stands, then consider your next step towards certification.

Product Certification

Show customers what has been checked

CNESS reviews a product against requirements for specific areas. A product is certified when it meets the requirements for the areas reviewed. This helps customers understand what has been checked.

Apply for Product Certification →

How CNESS helps

  • Make clear what the product review covers.
  • Answer customer questions with information from the review.
  • Identify areas where the product can improve.

How you can use it

  • Seek reviews for more products or additional areas when needed.
  • Use customer feedback and later reviews to keep the information useful.

What you gain

  • A clearer way to explain the care behind your product.
  • Information that can help new customers consider it.

What this means for your business

Give customers reasons they can understand when deciding whether your product is right for them.

Explain what customers get for the price

Customers want to understand what they are paying for. Information about the qualities covered by your product certification can help explain its value. They can then make a more informed choice.
