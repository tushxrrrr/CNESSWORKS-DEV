Take ISO 9001

It says a lot about quality. It does not say everything.

If you run a serious quality system, that matters. It tells us you can deliver consistently, keep improving, and look after the customer experience.

It does not, on its own, tell us how you treat people, what you do for your community, whether your product is kind to the planet, or whether integrity holds across the whole business.

Take B Corp

A strong signal — not a finished score

Being a B Corp is something we take seriously. It speaks to trust, responsibility, greater good, accountability and how you treat the people around you.

We still check the scope and whether it is current. And we may still ask for more, because one framework should never become a shortcut to “we’re done.”

Core Pillars of the CNESS Framework

    Conscious Leadership

    Leading with Values, Accountability, Governance Integrity & the Greater Good

    Conscious leadership establishes the foundation for every organization. It reflects how leaders guide with purpose, make responsible decisions, uphold governance, and create a culture of trust, accountability, and long-term value.

    1. 1. Purposeful Vision – Lead with clarity, values, purpose, and long-term responsibility.
    2. 2. Conscious Decision-Making – Make intentional decisions with wisdom and fortitude that positively impact people, culture, and the future.
    3. 3. Accountability & Ownership – Take responsibility for decisions, actions, and measurable outcomes.
    4. 4. Ethical Governance – Embed transparency, integrity, trust, and responsible stewardship throughout the organization.
    5. 5. People-Centered Leadership – Inspire individuals to achieve extraordinary results through empowerment, alignment, and shared purpose.
    6. 6. Resilient Organizational Impact – Build a culture of trust, excellence, sustainable growth, and enduring value.

    Human-Centric Value & Team Cohesion

    Enhancing Collaboration through Alignment, Trust & Shared Purpose

    Great organizations are built by people who feel valued, trusted, and connected to a common purpose. This pillar focuses on creating an environment where individuals grow, collaborate, and contribute to collective success.

    1. 1. Purposeful People – Unite individuals around a meaningful vision, shared values, and collective success.
    2. 2. Trust & Psychological Safety – Foster transparency, respect, wellbeing, and an environment where people thrive.
    3. 3. Collaboration & Team Cohesion – Encourage open communication, shared responsibility, accountability, and teamwork.
    4. 4. Empowerment & Growth – Invest in continuous learning, leadership development, and human potential.
    5. 5. Culture of Belonging – Value every individual and cultivate healthy, respectful workplace relationships.
    6. 6. High-Performing Teams – Build resilient, adaptable teams that achieve excellence through unity and conscious collaboration.

    Products & Services

    Driving Responsible Innovation & Long-Term Value Creation

    Products and services reflect an organization's purpose in action. This pillar recognizes organizations that innovate responsibly, create meaningful solutions, and deliver lasting value to customers and society.

    1. 1. Purposeful Innovation – Create solutions that address real needs and deliver meaningful value.
    2. 2. Customer-Centric Excellence – Design products and services that enhance user experience, satisfaction, and trust.
    3. 3. Quality & Reliability – Uphold the highest standards of performance, consistency, safety, and dependability.
    4. 4. Responsible Value Creation – Develop offerings that positively benefit customers, society, and the wider ecosystem.
    5. 5. Sustainable Differentiation – Innovate with integrity to create lasting competitive advantage and relevance.
    6. 6. Enduring Impact – Deliver products and services that strengthen trust, inspire loyalty, and sustainable growth.

    Customers & Clients

    Building Trust Through Quality, Dependability, Reliability & Meaningful Relationships

    Long-term success is built through trusted relationships. This pillar recognizes organizations that consistently deliver value, communicate with integrity, and cultivate enduring partnerships.

    1. 1. Purposeful Relationships – Build enduring partnerships founded on trust, respect, usefulness, and shared success.
    2. 2. Customer-Centric Excellence – Deliver consistent quality, value, responsiveness, dependable after-sales support, and exceptional customer experiences.
    3. 3. Transparency & Integrity – Communicate honestly, set clear expectations, and honor every commitment.
    4. 4. Engagement & Responsiveness – Listen actively, embrace feedback, and continuously improve products, services, and customer experiences.
    5. 5. Trust & Accountability – Take ownership of customer outcomes and strengthen confidence through reliable performance.
    6. 6. Enduring Loyalty & Growth – Foster long-term relationships that create mutual value, advocacy, and sustainable success.

    Community & Conscious Social Impact

    Connecting Business Success with Positive Societal Contribution

    Conscious organizations recognize that lasting success includes creating positive value for society. This pillar measures how purpose is translated into meaningful, measurable, and sustainable contributions to communities and the greater good.

    1. 1. Purposeful Contribution – Advance the greater good through meaningful and sustained community engagement.
    2. 2. Conscious Responsibility – Integrate social, environmental, and ethical responsibility into everyday business practices.
    3. 3. Community Partnerships – Collaborate with communities, institutions, and stakeholders to create lasting positive outcomes.
    4. 4. Empowered Participation – Encourage employees to contribute through volunteerism, service, innovation, and shared responsibility.
    5. 5. Sustainable Impact – Deliver measurable, long-term benefits that strengthen communities and build public trust.
    6. 6. Shared Prosperity & Legacy – Create enduring value that uplifts society, supports future generations, and reflects the organization's higher purpose.

    Building Resilient Brands Through Alignment & Strategic Partnerships

    A resilient brand emerges when purpose, performance, and partnerships are aligned. This pillar recognizes organizations that inspire trust, cultivate strategic collaborations, and create lasting influence through authentic leadership.

    1. 1. Purposeful Brand Vision – Define and communicate a clear purpose, values, and long-term direction that inspire trust and confidence.
    2. 2. Authentic Brand Identity – Align leadership, culture, operations, and customer experiences to consistently reflect the organization's purpose.
    3. 3. Strategic Partnerships – Build meaningful collaborations that foster innovation, shared value, and sustainable growth.
    4. 4. Reputation & Credibility – Strengthen brand trust through integrity, consistency, transparency, and responsible leadership.
    5. 5. Global Influence & Adaptability – Expand reach through collaboration, innovation, resilience, and responsiveness to a changing world.
    6. 6. Enduring Legacy – Create a brand that positively influences industries, communities, and future generations through conscious leadership and shared purpose.

Frequently Asked Questions about Sustainability Certification for Companies

Find answers about CNESS certification, eligibility, pricing, and the conscious enterprise journey.

What is CNESS Certification?

CNESS Certification recognizes who your organization is, what it stands for, and how it creates value across six key pillars. It provides a structured pathway to strengthen credibility, amplify growth, and become recognized as a Global Conscious Organization.

How do I get certified?

The process is simple. Create your organization profile, complete the required certification steps, and submit your information for evaluation. Through the process of assessing your organization, you also strengthen your practices, build your brand, and advance your organizational growth.

Will my existing certifications affect my eligibility?

No. Existing certifications do not affect your eligibility for CNESS Certification. They can complement the CNESS framework, add value to your organization's profile, and contribute positively to its overall recognition.

What is CNESS?

CNESS is a global Consciousness Ecosystem that connects and recognizes Global Conscious Organizations. It helps organizations: • Build credibility and trust • Strengthen global visibility • Demonstrate conscious business practices • Connect with aligned organizations and stakeholders • Create long-term value and measurable impact

What is CNESSWORKS?

CNESSWORKS is the digital platform where organizations begin their certification journey, create their Trust Profile, and become visible in the Global Directory. It brings certification, recognition, visibility, and access to a global ecosystem together in one platform.

Who can use CNESSWORKS?

CNESSWORKS is designed for businesses, institutions, startups, service providers, and organizations of every size. Organizations seeking to become recognized conscious organizations can use the platform to strengthen trust, improve credibility, and showcase their impact.

What is the Global Directory?

The CNESSWORKS Global Directory is where organizations showcase their conscious profile, credibility, certification status, and Consciousness Impact Score. Being listed connects your organization to the global community of Global Conscious Organizations (GCO).

Why should my organization be listed in the Global Directory?

The Global Directory features organizations recognized for their dependability and conscious practices across the six CNESS pillars. A listing helps your organization: • Build greater credibility and visibility • Connect with the global conscious business community • Reach new customers across international markets • Serve conscious customers around the world • Attract aligned professionals and strengthen talent acquisition • Enhance brand promotion, discoverability, and business opportunities

Do I need to be certified to appear in the Global Directory?

Organizations may appear in the Global Directory before completing certification. However, certification unlocks greater credibility, enhanced visibility, access to the Consciousness Impact Score, and recognition as a Global Conscious Organization.

What is the CNESSWORKS Partner Program?

The CNESSWORKS Partner Program enables individuals, organizations, institutions, and platforms to support conscious business growth through referrals, media, implementation, and strategic collaboration. It empowers partners to become a meaningful part of the positive change they wish to see in the world.

Who can become a CNESSWORKS Partner?

Conscious pioneers, prominent individuals, consultants, advisors, media platforms, institutions, service providers, and organizations creating meaningful impact can apply. The program is designed for those who can introduce, support, promote, or guide organizations within the CNESSWORKS ecosystem.

How can I become a partner?

Becoming a partner is a simple process. Select the pathway that best matches your expertise, network, and goals, submit your application, and the CNESSWORKS team will review it and guide you through the next steps.

Can A Product Embody Consciousness?

A product is not conscious in the human sense. Yet every decision embedded within it can reflect awareness, responsibility, dignity, transparency and long-term care.

Every material selected, person involved, process controlled, claim communicated, customer supported and impact created reveals something about the consciousness behind the product.

CNESS Conscious Product Certification examines whether responsible intent has become visible through credible practices, appropriate evidence, trustworthy outcomes and a commitment to improvement.

This is more than a certification badge. It is an opportunity to demonstrate that your organization creates not only commercial value, but responsible and conscious value for humanity.

What Does CNESS Evaluate?

Seven Foundational Pillars

The CNESS Conscious Product Certification evaluates how consciously a product is designed, sourced, manufactured, packaged, and presented across seven pillars totalling 700 points.

  • 01Product Integrity And Safety

    Built to fulfil its intended purpose. Designed to reduce avoidable harm. Governed responsibly as it changes.

  • 02Human Value And Wellbeing

    Created with people in mind. Respectful of dignity, access and wellbeing. Considerate of everyone it touches.

  • 03Ethical Sourcing & Responsible Value Chain

    Know where it comes from. Choose partners with care. Create through fair and responsible practice.

  • 04Environmental Responsibility & Sustainability

    Use resources with restraint. Reduce waste and avoidable impact. Consider the whole lifecycle.

  • 05Quality Performance & Lifecycle Value

    Do what it promises. Deliver consistently and reliably. Learn, improve and endure.

  • 06Customer Trust & Support

    Speak clearly and truthfully. Make choice and use understandable. Stand by the customer after the sale.

  • 07Utility & Pain-Point Solutions

    Create value beyond the transaction. Understand the wider consequences. Improve impact with purpose and humility.

  • Total Certification Score: 700 Points

Why Certify your Product?

One Certification. Transparent Evidence.

The public result should help customers and partners understand the certified scope, seven-pillar strengths, important limitations, evidence basis, validity and current status. The internal CIS-P 700 result supports evaluator consistency and improvement; it should not be used as a simplistic ranking across unrelated products, markets or sectors.

Begin your Product Certification Journey

  1. Define the exact product, service, component, software version, subscription or approved product family.
  2. Confirm legal eligibility and the applicable certification boundary.
  3. Complete the universal core questions across the seven pillars.
  4. Attach the most relevant evidence available for each material answer.
  5. Respond only to targeted evaluator clarifications or evidence gaps
  6. Receive an independent review and certification decision.
  7. Publish a time-bound Product Trust Profile and follow the Conscious Improvement Roadmap.

CNESS awards one public status: CNESS Conscious Product Certified™. Products that are not yet ready receive a confidential improvement pathway and may return after corrective action.
